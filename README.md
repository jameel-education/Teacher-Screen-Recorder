# Teacher Screen Recorder + Clip Combiner

## Free Windows Video Lesson Recording Software for Teachers

## Version 1.0.0 | First Stable Public Release: 13 November 2026

Teacher Screen Recorder + Clip Combiner is a free Windows desktop application developed to help teachers, educators and other users create recorded lessons, demonstrations, presentations and educational videos.

The software focuses on video lesson recording, with additional tools for preparing narration and combining short recordings into longer lessons.

It is designed to be simple, practical and accessible to teachers without requiring commercial video recording software.

## Main Features

- **Screen Recording:** Record classroom demonstrations, presentations, teaching boards and other on-screen activities.

- **Webcam Overlay:** Include an optional webcam view in recorded lessons.

- **Background Blur:** Adjust webcam background blur.

- **Microphone Recording:** Record voice narration with audio synchronisation controls.

- **Narration from Audio Files:** Create screen recordings using previously recorded narration.

- **Separate Audio Recorder:** Prepare and save narration files for later use.

- **Audio Enhancement:** Remove background noise and enhance voice recordings.

- **Clip Combiner:** Join short video clips into one longer, continuous lesson video.

- **Mouse Highlighting**: Make the mouse pointer easier to follow during demonstrations.

- **Local Processing:** Record and process videos directly on the user's computer.

## Clip Combiner

Clip Combiner is a simple utility included with Teacher Screen Recorder.

Its purpose is to **join shorter recorded clips into a longer continuous video**, particularly when teachers prepare lessons in several recording sessions.

It is not intended to replace dedicated video editing software.

## Minimum System Requirements

- **Operating System:** Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit).

- **Processor:** 64-bit dual-core processor or better.

- **Memory (RAM):** 4 GB minimum; 8 GB recommended.

- **Storage:** At least 1 GB for installation.

- **Webcam:** Optional; required for webcam recording.

- **Microphone:** Optional; required for voice recording.

- **Internet Connection:** Not required for normal operation.

- **Administrator Privileges:** Not normally required.

Performance may vary depending on computer specifications and recording settings.

## Recording and Processing Storage

Video recording and processing require additional temporary disk space.

As a practical estimate, a 10-minute recording may require approximately **5 GB of free disk space** during recording and processing. The final MP4 file is generally much smaller.

Longer recordings may require substantially more temporary storage. Users should ensure sufficient free space before recording.
